Generative search engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Google AI Overviews do not rank pages by traditional PageRank algorithms alone. Instead, they use complex information retrieval pipelines to select citations based on factual credibility, passage conciseness, and corroborated external entities.

Here is the technical playbook for making your digital assets citeable by AI search engines.

---

## 1. Provide Distinct Machine-Readable Facts

Generative engines favor clear, unambiguous declarations:
* Explicitly state company location, founding team, core services, and tech stack in machine-readable format.
* Maintain a root [llms.txt](/llms.txt) and [llms-full.txt](/llms-full.txt) specifying your verified technical capabilities.
* Use unambiguous numerical benchmarks (e.g. *"sub-800ms voice turnaround"*, *"95% reduction in data prep time"*) rather than vague adjectives (*"extremely fast"*, *"revolutionary"*).

---

## 2. Inverted Pyramid Writing: The 35-Word Definition Snippet

When answering technical questions, always place a concise 30-to-40 word self-contained summary directly beneath the section header. This provides retrieval algorithms with an easily extractable chunk that directly satisfies the user's conversational intent.

---

## 3. High-Density Domain Schema

Implement complete JSON-LD schema graphs connecting your Organization to specific Services, Case Studies, and author profiles. AI systems cross-reference these structured entities across LinkedIn, GitHub, and official registries to calculate source trustworthiness.
