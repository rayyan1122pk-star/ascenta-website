For customer-facing businesses—dental clinics, real estate brokerages, home service contractors, and digital agencies—phone calls remain the highest-converting customer channel. Yet maintaining dedicated human staff for after-hours calls or instant lead callback is economically challenging.

The emergence of low-latency voice AI systems allows businesses to answer inbound calls and execute outbound qualification in under 800 milliseconds. Here is an honest operational comparison.

---

## Cost Structure: Per-Minute vs Full-Time Equivalent (FTE)

| Dimension | Human Call Center Rep (US/UK) | Offshore Human Agent | Low-Latency Voice AI Agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Cost** | \$3,000 to \$4,500 / month | \$800 to \$1,400 / month | \$0.08 to \$0.14 / minute (~$150/mo typical usage) |
| **Availability** | 8 hours / day (shifts required) | Shift-based (limited 24/7) | 24/7/365 instant pick-up |
| **Concurrent Call Capacity** | 1 caller per rep | 1 caller per rep | Unlimited parallel calls |
| **Ramp & Training Time** | 2 to 4 weeks | 3 to 6 weeks | 1 to 2 days (prompt & knowledge base tuning) |

For a clinic or service business receiving 400 calls per month, a voice AI assistant costs approximately \$120 to \$200/month in compute and telephony—a 90%+ cost reduction compared to human staffing.

---

## When Voice AI Wins (And Where It Struggles)

### Where Voice AI Outperforms:
1. **Instant After-Hours Lead Capture:** Answering the phone on the second ring at 11:00 PM when a homeowner's AC breaks down, taking their address, and scheduling a technician.
2. **Speed-to-Lead Follow-Up:** Dialing web form leads within 15 seconds of submission to verify details before competitors can respond.
3. **Repetitive Booking & FAQ Calls:** Rescheduling appointments, confirming directions, and explaining service pricing packages.

### Where Humans Remain Essential:
* Emotionally charged customer disputes or refunds.
* Complex consultative sales negotiations involving multi-variable enterprise contracts.
* Highly nuanced medical triage requiring human clinical licenses.
