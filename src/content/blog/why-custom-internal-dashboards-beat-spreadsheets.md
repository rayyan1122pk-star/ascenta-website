Google Sheets is the most popular software prototype engine in the world. It is free, flexible, and lets non-technical founders track initial sales, inventory, and operations.

However, once a business reaches multiple team members or hundreds of weekly transactions, spreadsheets transform from an asset into an operational bottleneck.

---

## 4 Failure Modes of Spreadsheet Operations

1. **Accidental Formula Destruction:** One team member sorts a column incorrectly or deletes a cell formula, silently corrupting reporting data across the entire sheet.
2. **Zero Granular Access Control:** In a spreadsheet, users either see the whole sheet or nothing. You cannot hide financial margins or client contact details from junior staff or external contractors.
3. **No Automated Audit Logs:** When records vanish or change mysteriously, it is impossible to trace who modified what.
4. **Mobile Usability Nightmare:** Field technicians or sales reps cannot comfortably enter complex records into tiny spreadsheet cells on mobile phones.
